## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| tsx non-configurable getters | tsx wraps ESM named exports as non-configurable getters (`{ get: [Function: get], set: undefined, configurable: false }`). `mock.method()` from `node:test` CANNOT replace these. CJS `Module._resolveFilename` preload scripts are also bypassed by tsx's ESM loader. | blocking | Use `__setDeps()`/`__resetDeps()` injectable deps pattern in modules that need test mocking. Do NOT use `mock.method()` on tsx-resolved modules. Do NOT use CJS preload scripts for ESM mocking. |
